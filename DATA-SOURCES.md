# Provisional Zimbabwe business and catalogue data

The project contains a development seed of real Zimbabwean brand names, provisional locations, and selected product-name references. Its purpose is to reduce blank-page work: an owner can search, select, confirm, correct, and claim a record rather than recreating everything manually.

## Source approach

Spotly does not treat public web content as verified merchant data. The release uses limited brand/product-name references from official manufacturer and retailer pages, including:

- OK Zimbabwe public retail/category pages
- National Foods official brand and product-family pages
- Dairibord official brands and product pages
- Colcom public product information

No product photographs were copied into the release. Prices, stock, barcodes, pack sizes, contacts, hours, exact locations, ownership, and image rights are not inferred. Reference catalogue entries start inactive and require business review.

## Status of seed records

Every seeded record is provisional and unverified. It may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, duplicated, or unsuitable for publication.

The dataset must not be treated as proof that:

- the business currently operates at the listed location
- Spotly has a relationship with the business
- the business endorses Spotly
- a logo or photograph may be reused
- prices, products, hours, contacts, branches, or ownership are current

## Required review

Before a listing is promoted or transacts:

1. Confirm the business brand separately from its exact locations.
2. Confirm source and retrieval details.
3. Confirm contact, hours, and location information.
4. Confirm image/logo rights or replace media with owner-supplied files.
5. Confirm claimant authority and evidence.
6. Confirm catalogue names, variants, pack sizes, prices, stock, barcodes, and branch availability.
7. Publish corrections and support removal requests.
8. Record the verification actor and timestamp.

## Seed controls

The administration area can:

- import or refresh provisional records
- migrate legacy branch-as-business records to the brand/location model
- search and edit business brands and exact locations
- mark claim and verification status
- hide, archive, or publish listings
- review business claims
- record source metadata
- keep imported references separate from owner-verified operational data

The public application clearly distinguishes unclaimed or provisional status where applicable.
