# Provisional Zimbabwe business data

The project includes a broad development seed of real Zimbabwean brand names and provisional city listings. The objective is to make onboarding easier by letting an owner search, select, confirm, edit, and claim a pre-existing record rather than starting from an empty form.

## Status of seed records

Every seeded record is marked provisional and unverified. It may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, duplicated, or unsuitable for publication.

The dataset must not be treated as proof that:

- The business currently operates at the listed location
- Spotly has a relationship with the business
- The business endorses Spotly
- A logo or photograph may be reused
- Prices, products, hours, contacts, branches, or ownership are current

## Required review

Before a listing is promoted or transacts:

1. Confirm the business identity and branch.
2. Confirm source and retrieval details.
3. Confirm contact and location information.
4. Confirm image/logo rights or replace with owner-supplied media.
5. Confirm claimant authority and evidence.
6. Confirm catalogue, prices, availability, and pickup terms.
7. Publish corrections and support removal requests.
8. Record the verification actor and timestamp.

## Seed controls

The administration area can:

- Import or refresh provisional records
- Search and edit records
- Mark claim and verification status
- Hide or publish listings
- Review business claims
- Record source metadata
- Keep imported records separate from owner-verified operational data

The public application clearly marks provisional/unclaimed status where applicable.
