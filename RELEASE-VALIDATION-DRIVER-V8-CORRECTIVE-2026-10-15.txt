SPOTLY DRIVER V8 — ONBOARDING / VEHICLE REVIEW CORRECTIVE RELEASE
Date: 2026-10-15

PURPOSE
This release corrects gaps found during hands-on Driver onboarding after the initial Driver V8 implementation.
It is a complete Next.js project, not a patch.

CORRECTIONS IMPLEMENTED
1. Driver entry/authentication
- driver.spotlyafrica.com now welcomes new applicants explicitly.
- The unauthenticated gate says Start or continue your Driver application.
- New users are told they can create a Spotly account during sign-in.
- Driver branding is used on the gate and the public Driver acquisition page remains one click away.

2. Zero-to-active application staging
- Step 1 identity/contact can save before any vehicle fields exist.
- Vehicle validation starts only after Work & vehicle has actually been completed.
- Initial motor vehicle setup now collects ownership, registration, year, make and model.
- Motorized work requires vehicle make/model and registration before continuing.
- Driver licence is an explicit onboarding requirement where the selected vehicle/work requires it.
- Licence, insurance and fitness evidence collect expiry information; reference numbers are supported.

3. Add another vehicle
- Replaced the old one-step vehicle creator with staged setup:
  Vehicle & work -> Required evidence -> Review & submit.
- Driver chooses the exact work capabilities requested for that vehicle.
- Required evidence is derived from vehicle + work type.
- Motorized vehicles cannot be submitted with only a registration string.
- A vehicle stays setup_required until the evidence stage is complete; only final submission creates the Driver Operations review item.
- A Driver can resume information_required/rejected setup rather than creating a duplicate.

4. Vehicle/document authority
- Registration, insurance and vehicle-fitness evidence are scoped to the exact vehicle ID.
- One vehicle's papers cannot approve another vehicle.
- Admin vehicle approval refuses to proceed until the exact required evidence is approved and current.
- New vehicle work types merge into the Driver's requested capabilities so required training is not stranded.
- Active Drivers can complete newly-required capability training from Account health.

5. Admin Driver Operations
- Admin Driver detail now renders every submitted vehicle, not only vehicles[0].
- Each vehicle shows requested work, evidence, status and review reason.
- Admin actions include Approve vehicle, Request setup and Reject vehicle.
- The live Driver Network now contains Vehicle reviews and Document reviews queues.
- The Admin Work inbox now surfaces driver-applications, driver-documents and driver-vehicles.
- Driver queue items deep-link to Mobility with the exact Driver opened.
- /mobility?driver=<id> now opens the Driver record automatically.
- Previously-created pending vehicles remain visible; if they were submitted under the old incomplete flow, Ops can Request setup and the Driver resumes that same vehicle.
