# Spotly open marketplace data

Spotly keeps **places**, **canonical products**, and **merchant offers** separate. A place may be discoverable before its owner joins Spotly. A canonical product may be searchable before any store publishes an offer. Price, inventory, pickup, delivery, bookings and other merchant-operated actions are only enabled by server-authoritative commerce capability checks.

## OpenStreetMap places

`zimbabwe-open-places.json` contains named commercial/service places sourced from OpenStreetMap for Harare, Bulawayo, Mutare and Gweru. Attribution: **© OpenStreetMap contributors**. OpenStreetMap data is licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Source/license: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Every record retains its OSM element ID, source URL, license status and import date. These listings are discovery records, not endorsements, merchant partnerships or live fulfilment promises.

## Canonical grocery products

`zimbabwe-open-products.json` combines Spotly-created generic grocery references with eligible Zimbabwe-tagged product identities from Open Food Facts. Open Food Facts database data is available under ODbL 1.0 and individual database contents under the Database Contents License. Source/license: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use

Open Food Facts product images are intentionally not bundled here. Merchant or Spotly-owned/licensed imagery must be used for transactional product presentation.

## Excluded sources

Retailer catalogues, Google Maps-derived bulk records, social-network directories and other sources without clear reusable-data rights are not part of the default open-data import. Existing historical research records marked `provisional_review_required` remain review-only until rights are confirmed.
