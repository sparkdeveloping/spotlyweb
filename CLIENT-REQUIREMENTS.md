# Client requirements before public production release

The application exposes administrative fields and switches for most of these items, but the client must supply or approve the real values.

## Critical credentials

- Firebase Admin service account
- First super-admin email
- Apple authentication configuration
- Firebase Phone Authentication readiness
- Web Push VAPID key
- App Check reCAPTCHA Enterprise key
- Paynow USD and/or ZiG integration credentials
- Resend API key and verified sending domain
- Production domain and DNS access
- Error monitoring and uptime provider

## Legal and company information

- Legal business name
- Trading name
- Registered address
- Company registration number
- ZIMRA/TIN details
- Privacy and terms contacts
- Privacy policy
- Terms of service
- Merchant agreement
- Refund and cancellation policy
- Acceptable-use policy
- Cookie/consent policy
- Data-processing terms

## Finance and payment policy

- Default payment recipient
- Merchant-specific recipient override policy
- Commission and fee structure
- Payout cadence, delay, minimum, reserve, and hold rules
- USD and ZiG settlement arrangements
- Refund, partial refund, dispute, chargeback, and reconciliation ownership
- Cash-at-pickup and bank-transfer confirmation procedures
- Fiscal invoice, receipt, VAT, tax, and accounting requirements

## Merchant and partnership operations

- Initial grocery pickup merchants
- Authorized organization owners and branch managers
- Branch addresses, contacts, hours, pickup locations, and capacity
- Verified catalogues, prices, stock model, and substitutions
- Claim evidence and low-risk/manual approval policy
- Source and media-rights approval for provisional listings
- Merchant service-level and escalation policy

## Support and communications

- Public support email
- Support phone
- WhatsApp number
- Support hours
- Response and escalation targets
- Emergency/escalation owner
- Support-agent accounts and roles
- Unlisted YouTube orientation video IDs
- Transactional email and notification copy
- Professional ChiShona and isiNdebele translations for critical flows

## Pilot acceptance

- Named pilot owner
- Pilot merchant cohort
- Pilot customer cohort
- Test devices and browsers
- Sandbox payment plan
- Support coverage during pilot
- Severity and release-blocking defect definitions
- Security/privacy/accessibility test approval
- Backup/restore exercise
- Incident and rollback plan
- Written launch approval
